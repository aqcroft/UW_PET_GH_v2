Utility Warehouse Partner earnings calculator and side income explorer

This independent calculator was created by Adrian Croft, an Authorised Utility Warehouse Partner. It is not an official Utility Warehouse tool. It models a conservative first 60 days using the official UW Partner compensation plan.

The guided journey uses 3-service homeowner customers at £250 each rather than the higher £300 4-service homeowner payment, so it is not a maximum-earnings illustration. It also demonstrates qualifying Fast Start, High Activity and Momentum bonuses. Actual earnings vary and are not guaranteed.

Can Utility Warehouse be a flexible side income?

The Authorised Partner model can be built alongside employment, self-employment, family life or other commitments. This tool helps explain how the current compensation structure can build across the first 30 and 60 days.
